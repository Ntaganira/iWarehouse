Inventory accuracy

Accuracy: units found where expected in the counts closed that month. Loss and damage: their value at cost against the average Inventory account. Turnover: cost of sales against the average stock value, as times a year. Delivery accuracy: signed delivery note copies without a shortage or damage. Reconciliations: daily reports signed off by the next working day and monthly stock reconciliations by the Settings' working day. Variances: resolved by their due day. Order-to-delivery: days from payment to the last hand-over or release. A dash: nothing to measure.
